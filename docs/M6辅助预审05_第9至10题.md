## Question 9: gold-sgd-repair-or-replacement

Original question: Goods sold under Directive (EU) 2019/771 are not in conformity. May the consumer choose between repair and replacement, and must that remedy be free of charge?

Reference answer A: The consumer may choose repair or replacement in order to have the goods brought into conformity, unless the chosen remedy is impossible or would impose disproportionate costs on the seller in the circumstances listed in Article 13(2). Where repair or replacement is used, it must be carried out free of charge, within a reasonable time after the seller is informed, and without significant inconvenience to the consumer.
