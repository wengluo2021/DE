## Question 20: gold-ucpd-pyramid-scheme

Original question: Under Directive 2005/29/EC, is a pyramid promotional scheme described in Annex I unfair only after a case-by-case assessment, or in all circumstances?

A reference answer: Annex I, point 14, covers establishing, operating or promoting a pyramid scheme in which a consumer gives consideration for the opportunity to receive compensation derived primarily from introducing other consumers rather than from the sale or consumption of products. Article 5(5) says the Annex I practices are unfair in all circumstances. The same list applies in every Member State and may be changed only by revision of the Directive.
