## Question 5: gold-crd-withdrawal-digital-content

Original question: Does Directive 2011/83/EU always give a consumer 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract for digital content that is not supplied on a tangible medium?

Reference answer A: No. Article 9(1) gives 14 days to withdraw from a distance or off-premises contract without giving a reason, but only where an Article 16 exception does not apply. Article 16(m) excludes that right once performance of digital content not supplied on a tangible medium has begun with the consumer's prior express consent and acknowledgment that the right of withdrawal is lost.

## Question 6: gold-crd-late-delivery-termination

Original question: Under Directive 2011/83/EU, the parties did not agree a delivery date. The trader misses the Article 18(1) delivery period. The consumer then sets an additional period appropriate to the circumstances, and the trader still does not deliver. May the consumer terminate, and what must the trader reimburse?

Reference answer A: Yes. If the trader does not deliver within that additional period, the consumer may terminate the contract. Upon termination, the trader must reimburse all sums paid under the contract without undue delay. This answer concerns that additional-period route.
