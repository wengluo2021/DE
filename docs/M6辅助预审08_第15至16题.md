## Question 16: gold-dcd-modification-conditions

Original question: A contract under Directive (EU) 2019/770 supplies a digital service over a period of time. The trader wants to modify it beyond what is necessary to keep it in conformity with Articles 7 and 8. Which conditions in Article 19(1) must be met?

Reference answer A: The contract must allow the modification and provide a valid reason for it. The modification must be made without additional cost to the consumer. The consumer must be informed of the modification in a clear and comprehensible manner. Where paragraph 2 applies, the consumer must also be informed reasonably in advance, on a durable medium, of the features and timing of the modification and of the right to terminate or of the possibility to keep the service without the modification.
