## Understanding in Chinese

The question states: no delivery date was agreed, the default delivery period has expired, the consumer has granted an appropriate additional period, and delivery still does not occur. A requires the answer that the consumer may terminate, and that after termination the trader must not unduly delay refunding all payments made under the contract.

S400/S800 state the termination path but do not give the refund rule; recommend partial. F800 applies the 14-day refund rule after withdrawal of a contract under Article 13 to termination for late delivery under Article 18, mixing up the applicable scenarios; recommend wrong. Even if the citation really contains 14 days, it cannot support the refund conclusion for this question.

F400 also omits the refund; the original AI draft is provisionally recommended as partial, but after the sentence on the right of termination it says "This rule does not apply to sales contracts where the trader has refused to deliver." What the original text excludes is the requirement to grant an additional period; where delivery is refused, the contract may be terminated directly, and it is not that there is no right of termination. This reference could change the grade of the whole answer, so it is referred to a human as a special dispute for actual judgment.

F400 claims that 0 sets out both legal branches at once, the agreed delivery date and the default delivery date, and the quotation begins at "out in paragraph 1"; the question gives only facts about the default period, and the branch on an agreed delivery date that is not shown cannot be supplied from the question facts; recommend not supported. F800 claims that 1 takes as its premise only the facts about the default period already given in this question, and the conclusion is complete within the quotation, so it may be supported under the confirmed premise of the question.

The last item of S400 states that none of the inputs contains a refund rule. This time its five actual input blocks were read separately, and indeed none of them contains Article 18(3); however, that claim is bound only to citation 5, which discusses other remedies, and a single block cannot support an assertion of absence across all inputs, so under the per-claim actual-binding standard it is recorded as not supported.

## Original question and A reference

> Under Directive 2011/83/EU, the parties did not agree a delivery date. The trader misses the Article 18(1) delivery period. The consumer then sets an additional period appropriate to the circumstances, and the trader still does not deliver. May the consumer terminate, and what must the trader reimburse?

> Yes. If the trader does not deliver within that additional period, the consumer may terminate the contract. Upon termination, the trader must reimburse all sums paid under the contract without undue delay. This answer concerns that additional-period route.
