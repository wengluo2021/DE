## Understanding in Chinese

The question asks whether there is always a 14-day withdrawal right. A's answer is no: the general 14-day period is subject to the exceptions in Article 16; the loss-of-right condition in Article 16(m) is that performance has actually begun, the consumer has given prior express consent, and the consumer has acknowledged that they thereby lose the withdrawal right.

F400 only states the general 14-day period and the existence of exceptions, without explaining the specific exception for digital content. S400 additionally states that Article 14 concerns digital content, but also does not give the conditions of Article 16(m). F800 states consent to begin and acknowledgment of loss of the right, but omits the general 14-day period; F800/S800 also do not explicitly state the actual commencement of performance and prior express consent required by A. Under the adopted standard of completeness of necessary facts, all four overall answers are recommended as partial.

This does not mean that every sentence they write is unsupported. The actual citations for the remaining 5 claims retain the corresponding sentences and are recommended as supported. The citation for F400's only claim retains the 14-day period and the Article 16 exception, but ends at off-premis; the claim is written as a complete distance or off-premises contract, and the support for the expanded scope requires a separate choice by an actual human.

The question already limits the case to distance contracts, and the cited segment fully shows the distance branch; whether to accept the completion of the truncated word off-premises can be judged according to specific semantic sufficiency, without relying on uncited complete blocks.

## Original question and A reference

> Does Directive 2011/83/EU always give a consumer 14 days to withdraw from a distance contract for digital content that is not supplied on a tangible medium?

> No. Article 9(1) gives 14 days to withdraw from a distance or off-premises contract without giving a reason, but only where an Article 16 exception does not apply. Article 16(m) excludes that right once performance of digital content not supplied on a tangible medium has begun with the consumer's prior express consent and acknowledgment that the right of withdrawal is lost.
