## Question 21: gold-utd-unfair-term-test

Original question: Under Council Directive 93/13/EEC, when is a contract term regarded as unfair, and when is a term treated as not individually negotiated?

Reference answer A: A term that has not been individually negotiated is unfair if, contrary to good faith, it causes a significant imbalance in the parties' rights and obligations under the contract, to the detriment of the consumer. A term is always regarded as not individually negotiated where it was drafted in advance and the consumer has not been able to influence its substance, particularly in a pre-formulated standard contract.

## Question 22: gold-utd-plain-language

Original question: A seller offers a consumer a written standard contract covered by Directive 93/13/EEC. What does Article 5 require for the wording, and how is doubt about a term resolved?

Reference answer A: The written terms must always be drafted in plain, intelligible language. If there is doubt about the meaning of a term, the interpretation most favourable to the consumer prevails. That interpretation rule does not apply in the procedures laid down in Article 7(2).
