## Question 7: gold-sgd-two-year-liability

Original question: Under the original text of Directive (EU) 2019/771, for how long is a seller liable for a lack of conformity that already exists when the goods are delivered? May a Member State provide a longer period, and may the period be shorter for second-hand goods?

Reference answer A: The seller is liable where that lack of conformity becomes apparent within two years of delivery. Without prejudice to Article 7(3), that rule also applies to goods with digital elements. Member States may maintain or introduce longer time limits than those in Article 10(1) and (2). For second-hand goods, Member States may allow the seller and the consumer to agree a shorter liability or limitation period than those in paragraphs 1, 2 and 5, but that shorter period must not be less than one year.
