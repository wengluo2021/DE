## Question 14: gold-dcd-conformity-remedies

Original question: Digital content covered by Directive (EU) 2019/770 and supplied in exchange for a price is not in conformity. What remedies does Article 14 provide, and when is termination unavailable because the defect is minor?

Reference answer A: The consumer is entitled to have the digital content or service brought into conformity, to a proportionate price reduction, or to terminate the contract, under the conditions in Article 14. Where a price is paid, the consumer may terminate only if the lack of conformity is not minor, and the trader bears the burden of proving that it is minor.

### F400

Q&A: `ask-f49a87d14e0340fc815b239038e8414b`; suggested answer: **wrong**.

A Article 14 explicitly lists bringing into conformity, proportionate price reduction or termination, as well as the non-minor limitation when paying the price and the trader's burden of proof; this group's insufficient provided no necessary facts, so wrong is recommended (erroneous refusal to answer), without removing the denominator.
